# Privacy policy: Rapid Trigger & SOCD Tester by HLPlanet

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The Rapid Trigger & SOCD Tester browser extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- The extension requests no browser permissions.
- It only reads key presses made while its own window (the toolbar popup or its tab) is focused. It cannot see what you type on other websites or in other apps.
- Test results are calculated in your browser and disappear when you close the window. The only thing saved is your best tap score, stored locally in your browser so it can be shown next time. It never leaves your device.
- The extension makes no network requests. The links to hlplanet.com only open the website when you click them.

Questions: contact us through https://www.hlplanet.com/
