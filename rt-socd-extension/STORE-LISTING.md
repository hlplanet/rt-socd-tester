## Detailed description

Find out what your Hall effect, TMR or magnetic keyboard really does, right from your toolbar.

RAPID TRIGGER TAP TEST
Tap one key as fast as you can for 5 or 10 seconds. You get taps per second, peak tap rate, average press length and the shortest release gap. Rapid trigger resets a key as soon as it starts rising, so the release gap is where it shows. A timeline shows every tap. Compare keyboards, or your own rapid trigger settings, and beat your best score.

SOCD / SNAP TAP TEST
SOCD (Snap Tap, Snappy Tappy, Rappy Snappy) makes the last of two opposite keys win when both are held. Keyboards swap the keys within a millisecond or two, faster than any finger, so the tester can tell whether SOCD is on or off. Hold A and tap D (or W/S, or the arrow keys) and you get a clear verdict plus a timeline of both keys.
